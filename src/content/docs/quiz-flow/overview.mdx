Quiz Flow is a short guided questionnaire. Instead of browsing and filtering on their own, visitors answer a few questions and see the records that best match their answers.

## How it works

1. You describe your directory, and FieldFlow uses AI to write a quiz with questions and answer options
2. Each answer option carries tags (keywords)
3. When you activate the quiz, FieldFlow uses AI to match those tags to your taxonomy terms
4. When a visitor finishes the quiz, they see your directory filtered to their answers, with the closest matches first

## When to use Quiz Flow

Quiz Flow helps when:
- Your directory has many records and visitors might feel overwhelmed
- Visitors don't know the right words to search or filter by
- You want to walk people through a decision, like "What kind of help do you need?"

## Example

A resource directory might ask:

> **What do you need help with today?**
> - Finding housing
> - Getting food assistance
> - Legal help
> - Finding a job

Based on the answer, the results show only related resources.

## Ways to show the quiz

| Option | What visitors see |
|---|---|
| **Quiz link** | A hosted quiz page at `https://getfieldflow.io/d/[projectId]/quiz` |
| **Quiz only** embed | The quiz on your site. When it's done, visitors go to a page path you choose, with their answers added to the URL |
| **Quiz + directory** embed | The quiz, then results in the same embed, with a **Start over** option |

For most sites, **Quiz + directory** is the simplest choice, because the results show up right in the embed. See [Embed Code](/publishing/embed-code/) for the embed options.

## Before you start

Quiz Flow works best when your directory already has [taxonomies](/filters-and-search/taxonomies/) with synced terms, because quiz answers are matched to those terms.

## Getting started

1. In the **Overview** tab, check **Show Quiz Flow link in Publish tab** and click **Save**
2. [Create a quiz](/quiz-flow/creating-a-quiz/) in **Configure** > **Quiz**
3. Activate it, which also builds the [tag mapping](/quiz-flow/tag-mapping/)
4. Share the quiz link or add a quiz embed from the **Publish** tab
