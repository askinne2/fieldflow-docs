---
title: Tag Mapping
description: How quiz answers connect to directory results.
---

Tag mapping connects quiz answers to your directory's categories. It tells FieldFlow which taxonomy terms go with each answer's tags.

## How it works

1. Each quiz answer option has **tags**, which are keywords
2. Your directory has **taxonomies** with **terms**, the categories from your sheet
3. The tag mapping links tags to terms

When a visitor picks answers, FieldFlow collects their tags, looks up the matching terms, and filters the directory.

## Mapping is automatic

When you click **Activate** on a quiz, FieldFlow uses AI to build the mapping. It looks at:
- The quiz's answer tags
- Your taxonomy terms
- A sample of your directory data
- The **AI context** from your project's **Overview** tab, if you filled it in

## Improve the mapping

If quiz results don't feel right:

1. Go to the **Overview** tab and fill in **AI context**. Describe your directory, for example: "Addiction recovery resources in Alabama for adults and families." Click **Save**
2. Make sure your taxonomies are set up and synced
3. Go to **Configure** > **Quiz** and click **Regenerate Mapping** on the active quiz

If you see "Could not generate mapping", check that your taxonomies and data are synced, then try again.

## How results are ranked

When a visitor finishes the quiz:
1. Their answer tags are collected
2. Each tag is turned into taxonomy terms using the mapping
3. Records with those terms are shown, with records matching the most terms first

## Tips

- Test the quiz yourself and check that the results make sense
- Fill in **AI context** before activating for better first results
- After adding new categories to your sheet, sync and then regenerate the mapping
