title: Creating a Quiz
description: Generate a quiz for your directory and activate it.
---

## Open the quiz builder

Go to your project's **Configure** > **Quiz** tab.

If you see **Quiz Flow is off**, go to the **Overview** tab, check **Show Quiz Flow link in Publish tab**, and click **Save**.

## Generate a quiz with AI

1. Click **Generate Quiz with AI**
2. Under **Describe your directory**, write a sentence or two about what's in your directory and who it's for. For example: "A directory of addiction recovery resources in Alabama for people seeking help for themselves or a loved one."
3. Click **Generate**

FieldFlow writes a title, questions, and answer options based on your description and your directory's categories. When it's done, you'll see **Quiz created. Review it below and click Activate when ready.**

There's also a ready-made template for addiction recovery directories. Use it instead of generating if it fits your directory.

## Review the quiz

Your quizzes are listed on the Quiz tab. Click a quiz to view its structure: its questions, answer options, and the tags on each option.

To change a quiz, adjust your description and generate it again.

## Activate the quiz

Click **Activate** next to the quiz.

- Only one quiz can be active per project. Activating a quiz turns off the one that was active before
- Activating builds the [tag mapping](/quiz-flow/tag-mapping/) that connects answers to your categories

## Tips for a good description

- Say what kinds of resources are in the directory
- Say who the visitors are
- Mention the area you serve, if it matters
- Keep it short and plain

## Delete a quiz

Click the delete button on a quiz and confirm.
