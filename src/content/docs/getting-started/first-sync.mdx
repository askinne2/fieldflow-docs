title: Your First Sync
description: Pull data from your Google Sheet into FieldFlow and check that it looks right.
---

Syncing reads your Google Sheet and stores a copy of the rows in FieldFlow so your directory loads quickly. Your spreadsheet is never changed.

## Map your fields first

A sync only copies the columns you've mapped. Before your first sync:

1. Go to **Configure** > **Fields**
2. Click **Fetch columns from sheet** to load your column headers
3. Review each column, then click **Save all mappings**

See [Field Mapping](/field-mapping/overview/) for details.

## Run your first sync

1. Go to the **Sync** tab
2. Click **Sync now**
3. Wait a few seconds. The page refreshes the status while the sync runs

When it finishes, the sync appears under **Recent syncs** with how many records were created, updated, and deleted.

## Check your data

- Open your directory from **View your directory** on the Sync or Overview tab
- Make sure your records and fields show up as expected

If a column is missing, check that:
- It's mapped in **Configure** > **Fields** and saved
- It has a header in row 1
- You ran a sync after saving the mapping

## What syncing means

- FieldFlow reads your Google Sheet and saves a snapshot of the mapped columns
- That snapshot powers your live directory
- When you change the spreadsheet, sync again to update the directory, by hand or on a schedule

## Troubleshooting

**The sync failed:**
- Make sure Google Sheets is still connected (check the **Overview** tab)
- Check the Sheet ID and worksheet name on the Overview tab under **Data source**
- Make sure the connected Google account can open the sheet

**Some rows are missing:**
- Your plan limits how many records each project can hold. Look for a **Row limit reached** warning on the Sync tab. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans/)
