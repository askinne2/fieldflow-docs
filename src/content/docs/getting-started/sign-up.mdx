## Create your account

1. Go to [getfieldflow.io](https://getfieldflow.io) and click **Get Started**
2. Follow the prompts to create your account with your email address
3. Verify your email and choose a password when asked

When you're done, you land on your **Dashboard**.

FieldFlow creates an organization for you automatically. It's named after your email's domain (or your email name if you use a personal email service like Gmail). Your projects, team, and billing all belong to this organization.

:::tip[Were you invited?]
If someone invited you to their team, or is [transferring ownership](/billing/transfer-ownership/) of their account to you, don't sign up here. Use the link in the email you received instead. Signing up separately creates a second, empty organization.
:::

## Start a free trial

New accounts start on the Free plan, which can't create projects or sync data. To build a directory, start a 14-day free trial:

1. Go to **Billing** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Upgrade plan**
3. Pick **Starter** or **Professional**

See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans/) for what each plan includes.

## Your first dashboard visit

The Dashboard shows a short getting-started checklist:

1. Create your first directory
2. Run your first sync
3. Publish and embed

You can dismiss the checklist at any time.
