---
title: Connect Google Sheets
description: Link your Google account so FieldFlow can read your spreadsheet data.
---

FieldFlow reads data directly from Google Sheets using a secure Google sign-in. Your data stays in Google. FieldFlow only reads it.

## Connect your Google account

You connect Google from inside a project, so [create a project](/getting-started/create-project/) first if you haven't.

1. Open the project and go to the **Overview** tab
2. In the **Google Sheets Connection** section, click **Connect Google Sheets**
3. Choose the Google account that owns or can open your spreadsheet
4. Allow FieldFlow to view your spreadsheets
5. You'll come back to FieldFlow and see **Google Sheets connected**

The connection belongs to your **organization**, not just one project. Once it's connected, every project in the organization can use it.

:::note
The Google account you connect must be able to open the spreadsheet. If the sheet belongs to someone else, ask them to share it with that Google account.
:::

## Finding your Google Sheet ID

Every Google Sheet has an ID in its URL. You need it when you create a project.

Open your Google Sheet in the browser. The URL looks like:

```
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPqRsTuVwXyZ/edit
```

The Sheet ID is the long string between `/d/` and `/edit`:

```
1aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPqRsTuVwXyZ
```

## Worksheet name

If your spreadsheet has more than one tab, FieldFlow reads from **Sheet1** unless you enter a different worksheet name when you create the project. The name must match the tab name exactly.

## What permissions does FieldFlow need?

FieldFlow asks for **read-only** access to Google Sheets. It can't edit, delete, or create spreadsheets. It reads your sheet and keeps a copy of the rows for your directory.

## Disconnecting

To disconnect, go to any project's **Overview** tab and click **Disconnect** in the Google Sheets Connection section. Because the connection is shared by the organization, syncs stop working for all of its projects until someone connects again.
