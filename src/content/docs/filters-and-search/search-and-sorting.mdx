## Search

Every directory has a search box. Visitors type and results update when they stop typing.

- Search looks for the text anywhere in a record's synced data
- It works together with filters, so visitors can search within filtered results

## Sorting

Visitors pick a sort from the toolbar. The options are:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Default** | The default order |
| **Name: A–Z** | By the card title field, A to Z |
| **Name: Z–A** | By the card title field, Z to A |
| **Recently added** | Newest records first |
| **Sort by [taxonomy]** | Appears for each taxonomy with **Enable sorting** turned on |

To change which field "Name" sorts by, change the **card title** in **Configure** > **Display**. To add a taxonomy sort, turn on **Enable sorting** for it in **Configure** > **Filters & Sorting**.

### Group by category

Visitors can also turn on **Group by category** in the sort controls to group cards under category headings.

## Pagination

Results are split into pages. Visitors can:
- Move between pages
- See the total number of results
- Change how many records show per page: 12, 20, 24, 48, or 96
