This page explains what visitors see when they use your directory, on the hosted page and in an embed.

## Fast search and filters

Most directories load all of their records at once when the page opens. After that, search, filters, and sorting update right away, with no waiting.

Very large directories load in parts instead. They still work the same way for visitors, but each change can take a moment.

## Search

Every directory has a search box at the top. Results update as visitors type.

- Search looks for the text anywhere in a record's synced data
- It works together with filters, so visitors can search within filtered results
- If your directory has [translations](/languages/overview/), a search finds records in either language. A visitor reading in Spanish can type an English word, and the other way around

## Results count and active filters

A bar at the top of the results shows how many records match, for example **Showing 12 of 140 records**. The bar stays at the top of the screen while visitors scroll.

When filters are on, they show in the same bar under **Active filters:** as chips. Visitors click a chip to remove that filter, or **Clear all** to remove every filter.

If nothing matches, visitors see **Nothing matches those filters.** with a hint to try a different search or fewer filters.

## Scrolling through results

There are no page numbers. More records appear as visitors scroll down. At the bottom they see **You've reached the end.**

:::note
Quiz results pages are the exception. The hosted quiz page and the **Quiz + directory** embed still show results in pages with **Previous** and **Next** buttons.
:::

## Filters on phones

On a phone, filters open in a panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen. Visitors can drag the panel down or swipe it away to close it.

## Sorting

Visitors pick a sort from the toolbar. The options are:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Suggested** | The default order |
| **Name: A–Z** | By the card title field, A to Z |
| **Name: Z–A** | By the card title field, Z to A |
| **Recently added** | Newest records first |
| **[Category name]** | Appears for each taxonomy with **Enable sorting** turned on, for example **Service Type** |

To change which field "Name" sorts by, change the **card title** in **Configure** > **Display**. To add a taxonomy sort, turn on **Enable sorting** for it in **Configure** > **Filters & Sorting**.

### Group results under headings

Next to the sort menu there's a switch named after your first filter category, for example **Sort by Service Type**. When a visitor turns it on, cards are grouped under a heading for each term in that category.

## Share a filtered view

On the hosted directory page, the web address updates as visitors search, filter, and sort. They can bookmark the page or copy the address to share exactly what they're looking at.

## What visitors can't change

Earlier versions let visitors switch the card style and hide fields from the toolbar. Those choices now belong to you:

- Pick the card style (Modern, Compact, or Detailed) in **Configure** > **Display** > **Card layout**. See [Card Layouts](/display/card-layouts/#card-layout)
- Choose which fields show on cards in **Configure** > **Display**
