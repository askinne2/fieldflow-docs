title: Automatic Sync
description: Keep your directory up to date on a schedule.

Automatic sync pulls fresh data from your Google Sheet on a schedule, so you don't have to remember to sync.

## Set up automatic sync

1. Go to your project's **Sync** tab
2. In the **Automatic sync** box, check **Enable automatic sync**
3. Choose an **Interval**:

| Interval | Good for |
|---|---|
| **Every 30 minutes** | Data that changes often during the day |
| **Every hour** | Active directories with regular updates |
| **Every 6 hours** | Directories updated a few times a day |
| **Every 12 hours** | Directories updated once or twice a day (default) |
| **Every 24 hours** | Directories updated daily or less |

4. Click **Save schedule**

## How it works

- FieldFlow checks every 5 minutes for projects that are due
- A project is due when your chosen interval has passed since its last successful sync
- The sync runs in the background, the same way a manual sync does
- If a sync fails, FieldFlow retries it up to 3 times
- If all tries fail, an email goes out (see below)

## Requirements

- Google Sheets must be connected for your organization
- Your field mapping should be saved in **Configure** > **Fields**

## Checking on automatic syncs

The **Recent syncs** list on the Sync tab marks each sync as **Manual** or **Scheduled**.

## Failure emails

Automatic syncs run on behalf of the person who connected Google Sheets for your organization. If that person has left, they run on behalf of the Owner.

That person gets an email when a sync fails after all retries, as long as **Email on sync failures** is on in their [Preferences](/billing/settings/#notifications).
