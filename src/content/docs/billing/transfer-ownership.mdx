Use an ownership transfer when someone else should take over your FieldFlow account. For example, an agency handing a finished directory to its client, or a staff member leaving the organization.

The transfer moves the whole organization. Nothing is copied or rebuilt.

## What changes and what stays the same

**Stays the same:**
- The organization, its projects, and all synced data
- Layouts, filters, quizzes, and settings
- API keys and embed codes, so directories already on websites keep working
- Public directory and detail page links
- The subscription and plan

**Changes:**
- The recipient becomes the **Owner**
- You (the previous owner) become an **Admin** and keep access
- The billing email on the subscription is updated to the new owner's email

:::caution[The payment method does not change]
The subscription moves with the organization, but the card on file stays the same. After accepting, the new owner should go to **Billing** > **Manage billing** and replace the payment method with their own.
:::

## Before you start

- Only the **Owner** can transfer ownership. Admins, Editors, and Viewers don't see this option.
- The recipient does **not** need a FieldFlow account. If they're new, they create one from the email you send them.
- You can have one pending transfer at a time.

:::caution[Tell the recipient not to sign up first]
The recipient should **not** create a FieldFlow account on their own before accepting. Signing up separately gives them their own empty organization. After they accept, their dashboard may open that empty organization instead of yours.

Ask them to wait for the transfer email and use the link in it. That link walks them through creating their account and accepting in one step.
:::

## Start a transfer

<Steps>

1. Go to **Team** in the left sidebar (under **Organization**).

2. Scroll to the **Transfer ownership** section at the bottom of the page.

3. Enter the recipient's email address.

4. Click **Transfer ownership**.

</Steps>

The section now shows **Pending — sent to** the recipient's email, with the date the offer expires.

The transfer does not happen yet. Nothing changes until the recipient accepts.

## What the recipient sees

FieldFlow emails the recipient a link to accept. What happens next depends on whether they already have an account.

### New to FieldFlow

1. They open the invitation email and click the link.
2. They choose a password (at least 8 characters) to finish creating their account. Their email is already verified by the link.
3. They see a page that says **Take over** your organization's name, with who sent the transfer.
4. They click **Accept and take over**.

### Already a FieldFlow user

1. They click the link in the email and sign in if asked. They must sign in with the same email address the transfer was sent to.
2. They click **Accept and take over**.

### After accepting

The recipient can rename the organization right away by changing the **Organization name** and **URL slug**. This step is optional. Click **Save & continue** to rename, or **Skip for now** to keep the current name.

To rename the organization later, contact [support@getfieldflow.io](mailto:support@getfieldflow.io).

The recipient can also click **Decline** instead of accepting. You'll keep ownership and can start a new transfer.

## Expiration

A transfer offer expires after **7 days**. After that, the link stops working. To try again, start a new transfer.

## Cancel a pending transfer

While the transfer is pending, go to **Team** and click **Cancel transfer**. The link in the email stops working.

A pending transfer is also canceled automatically if you remove the recipient or yourself from the team.

## After the transfer

- The new owner controls billing, the team, and future transfers.
- As an Admin, you can still work on projects and manage the team, unless the new owner removes you.
- Once accepted, the transfer can't be undone from your side. To get ownership back, the new owner has to transfer it to you.

## Troubleshooting

**"An ownership transfer is already pending for this organization."**
Cancel the pending transfer first, then start a new one.

**"This transfer was sent to a different email address."**
The recipient is signed in with a different email. They should sign out and sign in with the address the transfer was sent to.

**"This transfer is invalid or has expired."**
The offer expired, was canceled, or was already used. Start a new transfer.

**The recipient accepted, but their dashboard shows a different, empty organization.**
This happens when the recipient already had their own FieldFlow organization. Your projects still appear in their project list, and the **Billing** page has an **Organization** picker. If they need help, contact [support@getfieldflow.io](mailto:support@getfieldflow.io).
