---
title: Managing Your Subscription
description: Start a trial, change plans, update payment info, or cancel.
---

Billing is per organization. Only the organization's **Owner** and **Admins** can manage the subscription. Editors and Viewers see the current plan but can't change it.

## View your current plan

Go to **Billing** in the left sidebar (under **Organization**). You'll see:
- Your current plan
- Subscription status, such as `trialing` or `active`
- When the current billing period ends

If you belong to more than one organization, pick one from the **Organization** menu at the top of the page.

## Start a trial or upgrade

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade plan**
3. Pick a plan and click its button

If you don't have a subscription yet, you'll go to Stripe checkout. New subscriptions start with a 14-day free trial.

If you already have a subscription, FieldFlow changes your existing plan in place. You won't be charged twice. Stripe prorates the difference. Changing plans during a trial doesn't restart or end the trial.

For the Enterprise plan, click **Contact Sales**.

## Manage billing in Stripe

Click **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal. There you can:
- Update your payment method
- View and download invoices
- Cancel your subscription

## Cancel your subscription

1. Go to **Billing** > **Manage billing**
2. Cancel the plan in the Stripe portal
3. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period

After that, your organization moves to the Free plan. The Free plan can't create projects or sync data. To sync again, subscribe again from **Billing**.

## Payment failures

If a payment fails, Stripe retries the charge. Update your payment method in **Manage billing** to fix it.

## After an ownership transfer

When ownership moves to someone else, the subscription moves with the organization. The card on file does not change. The new owner should open **Manage billing** and add their own payment method. See [Transferring Account Ownership](/billing/transfer-ownership/).
