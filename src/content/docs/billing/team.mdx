---
title: Team Management
description: Invite team members, understand roles, and manage who has access.
---

FieldFlow supports team collaboration. Several people can work on the same organization's projects, layouts, and syncs.

Go to **Team** in the left sidebar (under **Organization**) to see everyone with access.

## Invite a team member

Owners and Admins can invite people.

1. Go to **Team**
2. In **Invite a team member**, enter their email address
3. Choose a role: **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**
4. Click **Send invitation**

The person gets an email with a link to join. If they don't have a FieldFlow account yet, the link lets them create one. They'll set a password to finish signing up. If they already have an account, they sign in and join.

Invitations expire after 30 days.

### Pending invitations

Invitations that haven't been accepted yet appear under **Pending invitations**, with their role and expiration date. Click **Cancel** to withdraw one.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything: billing, inviting and removing members, all projects, and [transferring ownership](/billing/transfer-ownership/) |
| **Admin** | Billing, inviting and removing members, and all projects. Can't transfer ownership |
| **Editor** | Create, edit, sync, publish, and delete projects. Manage API keys. Can't manage billing or the team |
| **Viewer** | Best for people who mainly need to review projects. Can't manage billing or the team |

Each organization has exactly one Owner.

## Change someone's role

To change someone's role, remove them and invite them again with the new role.

To make someone else the Owner, use [Transferring Account Ownership](/billing/transfer-ownership/).

## Remove a team member

Owners and Admins can remove members.

1. Go to **Team**
2. Find the member
3. Click **Remove** and confirm

Removing a member takes away their access right away. Their FieldFlow login still works. They just can't see your organization's projects anymore.

The Owner can't be removed. You also can't remove yourself.

## Transfer ownership

The Owner can hand the organization to someone else from the **Transfer ownership** section at the bottom of the Team page. See [Transferring Account Ownership](/billing/transfer-ownership/).

## Belonging to more than one organization

A person can be a member of more than one organization. Each organization has its own projects, billing, and team.

- Your project list shows projects from every organization you belong to.
- The **Billing** page has an **Organization** picker when you belong to more than one.
- Other pages, such as **Team** and **Preferences**, show one organization at a time. If you need to work in a different organization, contact [support@fieldflow.io](mailto:support@fieldflow.io).
