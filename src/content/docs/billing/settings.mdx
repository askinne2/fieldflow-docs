Your personal settings live under **Account** in the left sidebar. There are two pages: **Profile** and **Preferences**.

## Profile

Go to **Account** > **Profile** to update:
- **First name** and **Last name**
- **Email** is shown but can't be changed here, because it's your sign-in identity

Click **Save changes** when you're done.

### Password

On the Profile page:
- If you don't have a password yet, click **Set a password**
- If you do, click **Change password** and enter your current password

Passwords must be at least 8 characters.

## Preferences

Go to **Account** > **Preferences** to set:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Default project** | Which project to open first, or None |
| **Timezone** | Auto-detect or a specific timezone |
| **Date format** | US (MM/DD/YYYY) or International (DD/MM/YYYY) |
| **Items per page** | 10, 25, 50, or 100 (for dashboard lists) |

## Notifications

Also on the Preferences page:

| Notification | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Email on sync failures** | On | Get an email when a sync fails after all retries |
| **Product updates** | On | News and feature announcements |

Sync failure emails go to the person who started the sync. For automatic syncs, that's the person who connected Google Sheets for the organization (or the Owner, if that person has left).

## Danger zone

At the bottom of Preferences:

- **Export data** downloads a JSON file with the synced records from every project you can access.
- **Delete account** permanently deletes your FieldFlow login. You'll type your email to confirm. This can't be undone.

:::caution
If you're the Owner of an organization, [transfer ownership](/billing/transfer-ownership/) before you delete your account, so someone can still manage billing and the team.
:::

To delete a single project instead, see [Create a Project](/getting-started/create-project/#delete-a-project).
