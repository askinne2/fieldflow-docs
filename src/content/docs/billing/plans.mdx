FieldFlow plans differ mainly in how many projects (directories) you can have and how many records each one can hold.

## Plan comparison

| | Free | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | $19 / month | $49 / month | Contact sales |
| **Projects** | 0 | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Records per project** | 0 | 1,000 | 10,000 | Unlimited |
| **Automatic sync** | — | Daily | Hourly | Every 30 minutes |
| Google Sheets sync | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Layouts, filters, search | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Detail pages and map view | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Quiz Flow | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Embeds and API keys | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Team members | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |

On your **Billing** page, Professional shows as **Pro** and Enterprise shows as **Agency**.

A record is one row in your Google Sheet. The header row doesn't count.

## Free plan

Every new account starts on the Free plan. You can explore the dashboard, but you can't create projects or sync data until you start a trial or subscribe.

## Starter

For a single directory. One project, up to 1,000 records, synced automatically once a day.

## Professional

For organizations with several directories or larger datasets. Up to 5 projects and 10,000 records per project, synced automatically as often as every hour.

## Enterprise

For agencies and large organizations. Unlimited projects and records, synced automatically as often as every 30 minutes. Email [hello@getfieldflow.io](mailto:hello@getfieldflow.io) to talk about pricing.

## Free trial

Starter and Professional include a **14-day free trial**. Start it from **Billing** > **Upgrade plan**, or from the [pricing page](https://getfieldflow.io/pricing). Checkout runs through Stripe and asks for a card. You won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

## What happens at the record limit

If your sheet has more rows than your plan allows, FieldFlow syncs the first rows up to your limit and skips the rest. The Sync tab shows a **Row limit reached** warning with a link to upgrade.

If you're at your project limit, creating a new project shows an error until you upgrade or delete a project.
