FieldFlow plans differ mainly in how many projects (directories) you can have and how many records each one can hold.

## Plan comparison

| | Free | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | $19 / month | $49 / month | Contact sales |
| **Projects** | 0 | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Records per project** | 0 | 1,000 | 10,000 | Unlimited |
| Google Sheets sync | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Fastest automatic sync | None | Every 24 hours | Every hour | Every 30 minutes |
| Layouts, filters, search | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Detail pages and map view | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Quiz Flow | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Embeds and API keys | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Team members | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Translated records, filter labels, extra languages | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Draft translations with Claude | No | No | Yes | Yes |

On your **Billing** page, Professional shows as **Pro** and Enterprise shows as **Agency**.

A record is one row in your Google Sheet. The header row doesn't count.

## Free plan

Every new account starts on the Free plan. You can explore the dashboard, but you can't create projects or sync data until you start a trial or subscribe.

If a paid plan ends and you move to Free, your directories stay online as they were, but syncing is paused. Nothing is deleted. Subscribe again to sync.

## Starter

For a single directory. One project, up to 1,000 records, and automatic sync once a day.

## Professional

For organizations with several directories or larger datasets. Up to 5 projects, 10,000 records per project, automatic sync as often as every hour, and translations drafted by Claude.

## Enterprise

For agencies and large organizations. Unlimited projects and records, and automatic sync as often as every 30 minutes. Email [hello@getfieldflow.io](mailto:hello@getfieldflow.io) to talk about pricing.

## Free trial

Starter and Professional include a **14-day free trial**. Start it from **Billing** > **Upgrade plan**, or from the [pricing page](https://getfieldflow.io/pricing). Checkout runs through Stripe and asks for a payment card.

You get a reminder email a few days before the trial ends. When it ends, your paid plan starts and your card is charged. Cancel any time before then to pay nothing. If you cancel, your directories and data stay intact.

## What happens at the record limit

If your sheet has more rows than your plan allows, FieldFlow syncs the first rows up to your limit and skips the rest. The Sync tab shows a **Row limit reached** warning with a link to upgrade.

If you're at your project limit, creating a new project shows an error until you upgrade or delete a project.
