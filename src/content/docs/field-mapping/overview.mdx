---
title: Field Mapping
description: Tell FieldFlow what each column in your spreadsheet represents.
---

Field mapping tells FieldFlow what your columns mean and which ones to use. A column called "Phone" probably holds phone numbers, but you confirm that here so FieldFlow can format it and make it clickable.

:::note
A sync only copies mapped columns. Map your fields and save them **before** you sync. If you change your mappings later, sync again.
:::

## Open the Fields tab

Go to your project's **Configure** > **Fields** tab.

## Load your columns

Click **Fetch columns from sheet**. FieldFlow reads the headers in row 1 of your sheet and lists each column.

FieldFlow guesses a type for each column from its header name. For example, a header containing "email" becomes **Email**, and one containing "website" or "url" becomes **URL**. You can change any guess.

Click **Preview sample data** to see a few rows from your sheet while you map.

## Choose a purpose for each column

Every column has a **Purpose**:

| Purpose | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Display field** | Synced and available to show on cards and detail pages |
| **Taxonomy** | Synced as categories, used for filters, sorting, and colored tags |
| **Hidden** | Not shown in the directory |

Turn the **Enabled** switch off to leave a column out. Use **Enable all** or **Disable all** to change a whole group at once.

### Display fields

For a display field, set:
- **Field Name**: a short internal name (for example, `email`)
- **Field Type**: how to format the value. See [Field Types Reference](/field-mapping/field-types/)

### Taxonomy fields

For a taxonomy, set:
- **Taxonomy Name**: an internal name (for example, `service_type`)
- **Taxonomy Label**: what visitors see (for example, "Service Type")
- **Type**: Flat, Multi-value, or Hierarchical
- **Display Style**: Badge, Chip, Text, or Hidden

See [Taxonomies](/filters-and-search/taxonomies/) for what each option does.

Click **Analyze taxonomies** to have FieldFlow look at your data and suggest which columns should be taxonomies, and which type to use.

## Save

Click **Save all mappings**. Then run a sync from the **Sync** tab so your directory picks up the changes.

## Tips

- You don't need to map every column. Only enable the ones you want
- Keep field names short and clear. They appear in the layout builder
- If a column holds several categories separated by commas, make it a **Taxonomy** with type **Multi-value**
- If you add columns to your sheet later, click **Fetch columns from sheet** again, then save and sync
