---
title: Map View
description: Show your directory records on an interactive map.
---

Map view adds a **List** / **Map** toggle to your directory so visitors can see records as pins on a map.

## Turn on map view

1. Make sure your sheet has location data. Use either:
   - One column with latitude and longitude, like `34.8526, -82.3940`, mapped as **Coordinates (map)** in **Configure** > **Fields**, or
   - Separate **Latitude** and **Longitude** columns
2. Sync your data
3. Go to your project's **Overview** tab
4. Check **Enable map view**
5. Click **Save**

Visitors now see a **List** / **Map** toggle in the directory toolbar.

## How the map works

- Records without coordinates still appear in the list, just not on the map
- Nearby pins are grouped together. Click a group to zoom in
- The map shows up to 100 records at a time
- Search and filters apply to the map too

## Marker colors

To color pins by category, set **Category colors** in **Configure** > **Filters & Sorting**. See [Taxonomies](/filters-and-search/taxonomies/#category-colors).

## Detail pages

When a record has coordinates, its [detail page](/display/detail-pages/) also shows a map card with an **Open in Google Maps** button.
