---
title: Detail Pages
description: Give each record its own page with contact info, directions, and hours.
---

Detail pages give each record in your directory its own full page. When a visitor clicks a card's action button, they see everything about that record.

## Turn on detail pages

1. Go to your project's **Overview** tab
2. Check **Enable detail pages**
3. Click **Save**

Each card then shows an action button (by default, "View Details") that opens the record's detail page. You can rename the button in **Configure** > **Display**.

## What detail pages include

### Header
The record title and its category tags.

Category tags appear in the header when the taxonomy uses the **Badge** or **Chip** display style and the record has 6 or fewer terms in it. Other categories appear lower on the page in a section you can expand.

### Sections
Your fields, grouped into the sections you set up.

### Contact & directions
Contact fields are gathered into a **Contact & directions** panel beside the main content, with click-to-call, email, and website links. On a phone, the panel appears below the main content.

If the record has coordinates, the panel also has a **Get directions** button that opens the location in Google Maps.

### Hours
A field named like "Hours" or "Schedule" shows with a clock icon.

## Set up sections

Go to **Configure** > **Detail Page**.

1. Under **Sections**, click **+** to add a section
2. Select the section to edit it in **Section settings**:
   - **Section name**
   - **Style**: Card, Plain, or Accordion
   - **Fields in this section**: check the fields to include
3. Sections appear on the page in the order listed
4. Use **Remove section** to delete one

The **Detail page preview** shows a real record from your directory with your draft sections and your saved colors. Click **Desktop** or **375px** to check it at desktop or phone width.

When you have unsaved changes, a bar appears at the bottom of the screen. Click **Save** to keep them or **Discard** to undo them.

The fields you can pick come from **Configure** > **Display**. If you added columns there, click **Refresh data fields**.

## Detail page URLs

On the hosted directory, each record has a shareable URL like:

```
https://getfieldflow.io/p/[projectId]/[recordId]
```

A record's URL is permanent. When you edit the row in your sheet and sync, the record keeps the same URL. Links shared before this change still work: they forward to the record's current address.

If rows in your sheet get inserted, deleted, or sorted, set a [record key](/syncing/record-key/) so each record keeps its URL.

In an embed, the detail view opens inside the embed, and the page address changes so visitors can share a link straight to that record. To send visitors to pages on your own site instead, see [Advanced Embed Options](/embedding/advanced-options/#detail-url-template).

## Buttons on the detail page

The hosted detail page has:
- **Back to Directory** (or **Back to results** if the visitor came from a quiz)
- **Share**: opens your device's share menu, or copies the link
- **Print**: opens a print-friendly view

## Translated detail pages

If your directory has [translations](/languages/overview/), a visitor reading in another language sees the translated text. Fields with no translation show in English. The page then shows **Information partially available in this language.**

If the English text changed after it was translated, the page shows **Some of this information may be out of date in this language.** See [Translate Your Records](/languages/translate-records/#out-of-date-translations).
