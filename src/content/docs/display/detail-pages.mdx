title: Detail Pages
description: Give each record its own page with contact info, map, and hours.
---

Detail pages give each record in your directory its own full page. When a visitor clicks a card's action button, they see everything about that record.

## Turn on detail pages

1. Go to your project's **Overview** tab
2. Check **Enable detail pages**
3. Click **Save**

Each card then shows an action button (by default, "View Details") that opens the record's detail page. You can rename the button in **Configure** > **Display**.

## What detail pages include

### Header
The record title and its category tags.

Category tags appear in the header when the taxonomy uses the **Badge** or **Chip** display style and the record has 6 or fewer terms in it. Other categories appear lower on the page in a section you can expand.

### Sections
Your fields, grouped into the sections you set up.

### Contact Information
Website, phone, email, and address fields are gathered into a contact card with click-to-call, email, and website links.

### Map
If the record has coordinates, a map card shows the location with an **Open in Google Maps** button.

### Hours
A field named like "Hours" or "Schedule" appears in an **Hours** card with a clock icon.

## Set up sections

Go to **Configure** > **Detail Page**.

1. Under **Sections**, click **+** to add a section
2. Select the section to edit it in **Section settings**:
   - **Section name**
   - **Style**: Card, Plain, or Accordion
   - **Fields in this section**: check the fields to include
3. Sections appear on the page in the order listed
4. Use **Remove section** to delete one

The **Detail page preview** shows how it will look.

The fields you can pick come from **Configure** > **Display**. If you added columns there, use the **Tools** box to sync the section structure with your current Display layout.

## Detail page URLs

On the hosted directory, each record has a shareable URL like:

```
https://getfieldflow.io/p/[projectId]/[recordId]
```

In an embed, the detail view opens inside the embed. To send visitors to pages on your own site instead, see [Advanced Embed Options](/embedding/advanced-options/#detail-url-template).

## Buttons on the detail page

The hosted detail page has:
- **Back to Directory** (or **Back to results** if the visitor came from a quiz)
- **Share**: opens your device's share menu, or copies the link
- **Print**: opens a print-friendly view
