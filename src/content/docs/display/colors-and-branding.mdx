You can adjust your directory's colors so it fits in with your website.

## Where to change colors

Go to **Configure** > **Display** and open the **Customize** panel. Color settings are spread across these sections:

### Header
- **Header style**: Flat color or Gradient
- **Header background** (flat) or a **Gradient preset**, with optional custom gradient start and end colors
- **Header text color**: leave blank to use automatic contrast

### Card Surface
- **Card background**
- **Card border**

### Buttons
- **Primary**: used for buttons, links, and accents
- **Button text**
- **Pills (secondary)**: leave blank to base it on the primary color

### Text
- **Label color**
- **Body text** color

Leave a color blank to use the default.

Click **Save layout** to apply your changes.

## Category colors

To color specific taxonomy terms (for example, a different color for each service type), go to **Configure** > **Filters & Sorting** > **Category colors**. These colors are used for map markers and filter pills. See [Taxonomies](/filters-and-search/taxonomies/#category-colors).

## Light and dark mode

Directories support light and dark mode.

- On the hosted directory, it follows the visitor's device setting.
- For embeds, you choose **System (follow device)**, **Light**, or **Dark** when you copy your [embed code](/publishing/embed-code/).

FieldFlow adjusts link colors in dark mode so text stays readable against dark backgrounds.

## Tips

- Pick a primary color with good contrast in both light and dark mode
- Check your directory in both modes before publishing
- Use the live preview in the Display tab to see changes right away
