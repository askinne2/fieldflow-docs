The layout builder controls what each record looks like in your directory. You choose the fields, their order, and the styling, and see a live preview as you go.

## Open the layout builder

Go to your project's **Configure** > **Display** tab.

## Card title

Pick the field to use as the **card title**. It's the main heading on each card and is what the **Name: A–Z** sort uses. It isn't repeated in the card body.

## Choose and order fields

The **Fields** list shows the fields from **Configure** > **Fields**.

- **Show or hide** a field with the eye button
- **Reorder** fields by dragging them
- **Custom link text**: for URL, email, and phone fields, you can enter text such as "Visit Website" or "Call Now" to show instead of the raw link. It's used on cards and detail pages

If you added or enabled columns in **Configure** > **Fields** and they don't show up here, click **Reset to default** in the **Layout Tips** box. This rebuilds the field list from your current field mapping.

## Customize

The **Customize** panel has these sections:

| Section | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Quick Start** | Apply a Modern, Classic, or Compact style preset |
| **Layout** | Grid columns (1 to 4) and card corner radius |
| **Header** | Flat color or gradient, header colors, and header text color |
| **Card Surface** | Card background and border colors |
| **Buttons** | Primary color, button text color, and pill color |
| **Text** | Label and body text colors, and title, label, and body text sizes |
| **Display** | Show field labels, show the action button, and the button label |

The **action button** (for example, "View Details") links to each record's detail page. It only appears when detail pages are turned on in the **Overview** tab. See [Detail Pages](/display/detail-pages/).

For more on colors, see [Colors & Branding](/display/colors-and-branding/).

## Live preview

The **Live Preview** updates as you make changes.

## Save

Click **Save layout**. Visitors see the new design the next time they load or refresh the directory.

## What visitors can change

Visitors can adjust their own view from the directory toolbar. Their choices are remembered in their browser and don't change your settings:

- **Card style**: Modern (the default), Compact, or Detailed
- **Fields**: show or hide optional fields
- **List or Map**, when [map view](/display/map-view/) is on
